    Tips for Finding a Free Toilet in Vienna

    Look near public parks, markets, U-Bahn stations and high-traffic squares. Some locations are open 24 hours, while others depend on staff hours or seasonal park schedules.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the data official?
    Base public WC data comes from Stadt Wien Open Government Data. Community notes and manual additions are added separately.
    Can I filter the map?
    Yes. The main map supports filters for free toilets, accessible toilets, baby changing rooms and likely open locations.
